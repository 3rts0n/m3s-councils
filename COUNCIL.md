# The M3S Council

Five perspectives. A decision you can defend.

A business decision method from Middle 3rd Solutions, explored through Boot Room Labs. Use it to expose assumptions, argue the alternatives, and identify the smallest useful test before committing money, people, or reputation. The human decision owner makes the call.

## Bring a decision

Use this for a partnership, pricing change, hire, market entry, product investment, or consequential vendor choice. If the question is a factual lookup, verify the fact instead of convening a council.

Paste or attach this method to your AI tool, then fill in what you know:

```text
Convene the M3S Council on this decision.
Decision:
Options, including doing nothing:
Desired outcome:
Facts and sources:
Assumptions and missing information:
Who else must say yes, and what they want:
Existing commitments and constraints:
Cost of being wrong, including attention and reversibility:
Decision deadline:
Decision owner:
Review date:
```

Use only information you are authorized to share with your chosen AI provider. The method does not change that provider's data policies.

## Instructions for the model

### 1. Frame the choice

Restate the decision, the realistic options, the desired outcome, and the cost of being wrong. Separate supplied facts, checked sources, assumptions, and unknowns. Ask one focused question only if a missing detail prevents a useful comparison. Otherwise proceed with clearly labeled assumptions. Never manufacture numbers, cases, quotes, or a person's incentives to fill a gap.

### 2. Declare the mode

Choose the strongest mode the actual platform supports and state which mode ran:

- Independent first pass: Use separate subagents or isolated model calls. Give every seat the same decision brief, the evidence, and only that seat's method. Do not pass other seats' answers or the chairman's preferred conclusion. Collect all five first passes before synthesis. Independent here describes the execution setup, not statistical independence or freedom from shared model bias.
- Single-model role pass: When separate execution is unavailable, reason through the five perspectives in one conversation. Label this explicitly as a single-model role pass. Do not describe it as independent or anonymous.

Do not invoke paid APIs, buy tools, or create external resources merely to achieve a stronger mode. Stay within the user's authorized tools and scope. If a first pass fails, disclose it; do not fabricate a completed seat.

### 3. Hear the five seats

Each seat supplies: its recommendation, the evidence or assumption driving it, the biggest risk, and the fact that would change its mind. Be concise and specific. Give each reasoning method a fair hearing. Preserve real disagreement without inventing conflict for theater.

| Seat | Method | The question it brings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Counterparty | Role reversal | What makes the other party commit, continue, or walk away? Infer incentives cautiously and label them as hypotheses. |
| Contrarian | Pre-mortem | If this fails, what is the most plausible cause? What is the strongest case for the rejected option or doing nothing? |
| CFO | Unit economics | Do the costs, margins, cash timing, and resource demands work at launch and after the first year? Use supplied numbers; show missing inputs and conditional math. |
| Operator | Execution backcast | What has to happen first, who carries it, and what breaks when this succeeds? Surface dependencies and the burden on the existing business. |
| Precedent | Case comparison | What verifiable comparable case helps, and where does the analogy fail? Cite available sources. With no checked case, say so, identify the comparison needed, and leave that evidence gap open. |

The seats are reasoning lenses, not real executives or experts. Do not invent biographies, professional experience, or access to a private corpus.

For partnership decisions, also examine incentives for both sides, the lightest workable commitment, the gap between the announcement and the operating agreement, and whether build, borrow, or buy better closes the underlying capability gap. Apply these questions where relevant; do not force partnership language onto an internal hire or pricing decision.

### 4. Review the arguments

Run one review round. Three anonymous reviews are appropriate only if the platform can actually create isolated reviewers and withhold answer authorship. Remove seat and model names, shuffle the answers, assign neutral labels, and keep the identity mapping outside reviewer context. Give each reviewer the shared brief, evidence, and anonymized arguments. If identifying content cannot be removed without changing the argument, disclose the limit and call the review non-anonymous.

The three reviewers examine:

1. Evidence: which argument is best supported, and which conclusion outruns its sources?
2. Blind spots: which assumption, stakeholder, or cost did every seat miss?
3. Opposition: what is the strongest case against the emerging consensus?

If real isolation or anonymization is unavailable, perform these three checks in the current conversation and label them **non-anonymous review checks**. Never imply that shuffling labels inside one conversation creates anonymous reviewers. Agreement is not proof; multiple seats may share the same error.

### 5. Give the chairman's recommendation

Synthesize the reasoning instead of counting votes. A conditional recommendation, a smaller test, or deferral for a decisive missing fact may be the right answer. Show a compact five-row seat readout first, with each seat's conclusion and the evidence or assumption driving it. Then return a compact decision brief with these fields:

- Recommendation: the option, the reason, and any conditions.
- Strongest objection: the best surviving argument against it.
- What changes the answer: a specific fact, threshold, or event. Label a proposed threshold as proposed.
- Success-case cost: the people, money, attention, and operating burden if this works.
- Smallest next test: one reversible action that reduces the most consequential uncertainty, plus the evidence to collect. Propose a success measure when none is supplied and label it as proposed.
- Owner and review date: use the user's supplied owner and date. If absent, mark them unassigned or proposed; never imply someone has agreed.
- Evidence and uncertainty: the key sources, unverified claims, and unresolved inputs.
- How this ran: independent first pass or single-model role pass; anonymous reviewers or non-anonymous checks; any failed seats or missing capabilities.

End by handing the choice back to the human decision owner. Do not send messages, sign agreements, spend money, alter records, or carry out the recommendation as part of this method. Stop at a reviewable decision brief.

## What this experiment tests

Can giving different reasoning methods an explicit job make business decisions more inspectable? Judge a run by the assumptions it exposes and the quality of its next test. Fluency, agreement, and confidence are not evidence of accuracy. This method does not guarantee better outcomes or eliminate bias.
